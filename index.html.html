1Introduction & Purpose

This Master Volunteer Ambassador Agreement (“Agreement”) is entered into between Nepali Leaders Network (“NLN”), operated by SKS Ventures LLC (“Organization”), and the individual signing this Agreement (“Ambassador”).

NLN appoints Ambassadors to serve as volunteer leaders responsible for helping establish, grow, and support local NLN chapters while advancing the Organization’s mission of helping ambitious Nepalis connect, grow, and give back.

Ambassadors represent NLN within their assigned communities, assist in planning and executing Organization initiatives, and contribute toward building a strong, professional network for Nepalis worldwide.

This Agreement establishes the standards, expectations, responsibilities, rights, and obligations applicable to every Ambassador. Specific responsibilities for each Ambassador role are outlined in the applicable Role Addendum, which forms part of this Agreement.

2Nature of the Relationship

The Ambassador acknowledges and agrees that:

  • This is a voluntary, unpaid leadership position.
  • Nothing in this Agreement creates an employment, independent contractor, partnership, joint venture, or agency relationship.
  • The Ambassador is not entitled to wages, salary, benefits, workers’ compensation, unemployment benefits, or future employment.
  • Participation is voluntary and may be terminated by either party in accordance with this Agreement.

Unless expressly authorized in writing, the Ambassador has no authority to enter into contracts on behalf of NLN, accept sponsorships or donations, make financial commitments, promise reimbursements, or bind NLN to any legal or financial obligation.

3Appointment Term

The Ambassador’s appointment period, assigned city, role, and expected participation shall be specified in the applicable Role Addendum.

Unless otherwise agreed, Ambassadors are expected to actively participate throughout their appointment by attending meetings, completing assigned responsibilities, supporting chapter initiatives, and assisting with Organization-approved events. Appointment renewal shall be at the sole discretion of the Organization.

4Roles, Responsibilities & Professional Expectations

The Ambassador agrees to:

Professional Conduct

  • Represent NLN professionally and ethically.
  • Uphold the Organization’s mission, values, and reputation.
  • Exercise integrity, accountability, and respect.

Communication

  • Respond to Organization communications within a reasonable timeframe.
  • Attend required meetings, planning sessions, and training.
  • Notify the Organization when unavailable.

Leadership

  • Take ownership of assigned responsibilities.
  • Collaborate respectfully with fellow Ambassadors and volunteers.
  • Support Organization initiatives in good faith.

Role-specific responsibilities are contained within the applicable Role Addendum.

5Confidentiality

The Ambassador agrees to keep confidential all non-public information obtained through participation with NLN. Confidential Information includes, but is not limited to: internal documents, SOPs, marketing strategies, Organization systems, financial information, member information, sponsor information, venue information, speaker information, future initiatives, internal meetings, internal communications, and proprietary processes.

The Ambassador shall not copy, disclose, distribute, or use confidential information for personal or third-party benefit. These obligations survive termination indefinitely.

6Intellectual Property

All work created by the Ambassador relating to NLN activities shall become the exclusive property of Nepali Leaders Network / SKS Ventures LLC. This includes documents, SOPs, templates, presentations, photography, video, graphics, marketing materials, branding, event assets, research, social media content, and strategic documents.

Portfolio use requires prior written approval.

7Media Release & Consent

The Ambassador grants NLN permission to use photographs, videos, recordings, testimonials, quotes, event footage, and similar materials for promotional, educational, and marketing purposes without additional compensation.

8Professional Representation & Use of Position

The Ambassador agrees to represent NLN professionally and ethically at all times, and shall conduct themselves respectfully with venues, speakers, sponsors, attendees, volunteers, and community members; follow NLN branding guidelines; and use the Ambassador title only for authorized Organization activities.

The Ambassador shall not:

  • Use their Ambassador position for personal financial gain.
  • Promote or sell personal businesses, services, products, investment opportunities, fundraising campaigns, or commercial activities through NLN without written approval.
  • Use Organization relationships or member information for personal benefit.
  • Speak on behalf of NLN without authorization.
  • Create unofficial NLN chapters, events, or programs.
  • Make sponsorship, financial, or contractual commitments on behalf of NLN.

Misuse of the Ambassador position may result in immediate termination.

9Organization Approval & Authority

Ambassadors shall perform their responsibilities in accordance with Organization policies and reasonable instructions. Unless expressly authorized in writing, an Ambassador may not:

  • Commit Organization funds.
  • Enter into agreements.
  • Approve expenditures.
  • Announce partnerships.
  • Launch Organization initiatives.
  • Establish official Organization policies.
  • Make decisions materially affecting NLN’s operations, finances, legal obligations, or reputation.

When uncertain, Ambassadors are expected to seek approval before acting.

10Venue Use & Property Responsibility

Ambassadors shall exercise reasonable care while using any venue or third-party facility, and agree to follow venue rules, respect venue property, maintain a clean and professional environment, and promptly report any incident or damage.

If intentional misconduct, reckless behavior, negligence, or failure to follow venue policies results in property damage, excessive cleaning fees, repair costs, venue penalties, or other financial losses incurred by NLN, the Ambassador may be held financially responsible to the extent permitted by law. NLN may rely upon venue reports, photographs, video surveillance, witness statements, or other reasonable evidence when determining responsibility.

11Expenses & Reimbursements

NLN may reimburse reasonable, necessary, and pre-approved expenses incurred directly for Organization-approved event planning or execution. Examples may include venue-related purchases, event supplies, printing, approved vendor expenses, and other Organization-approved event costs.

Unless expressly approved in writing, NLN will not reimburse personal expenses, including: rideshare services (Uber, Lyft, taxis), personal mileage, fuel, parking, meals or beverages for the Ambassador, lodging, personal equipment, personal technology purchases, or any expense incurred without prior approval.

Receipts and supporting documentation may be required for reimbursement.

12Organizational Assets & Property

All Organization assets remain the exclusive property of NLN. This includes social media accounts, email accounts, Canva, Google Drive, ClickUp, WhatsApp groups, Luma, speaker databases, sponsor contacts, venue contacts, documents, templates, event materials, and member information. No ownership rights are transferred to the Ambassador.

13Conflict of Interest

The Ambassador shall avoid activities that conflict with NLN’s interests. Without written approval, the Ambassador shall not:

  • Use the Ambassador role for personal business.
  • Recruit members, sponsors, or partners away from NLN.
  • Accept compensation related to Organization activities from third parties.
  • Operate competing initiatives that materially conflict with NLN while serving as an Ambassador.

Potential conflicts shall be promptly disclosed.

14Community Standards & Code of Conduct

The Ambassador agrees to:

  • Treat everyone with dignity and respect.
  • Foster a welcoming, inclusive environment.
  • Avoid harassment, discrimination, intimidation, or retaliation.
  • Comply with all applicable federal, state, and local laws.
  • Follow venue policies and Organization guidelines.
  • Maintain professionalism during all Organization activities.

Violation may result in immediate removal.

15Technology, Data & Information Security

The Ambassador agrees to protect Organization accounts and passwords, use Organization systems responsibly, not copy or export Organization data without authorization, not share Organization credentials, and report any security issue immediately.

Unauthorized use may result in immediate termination.

16Social Media & Public Communications

The Ambassador agrees to represent NLN accurately, protect confidential information, not publish internal documents or communications, obtain approval before making official Organization announcements, and avoid communications that may reasonably damage NLN’s reputation.

17Non-Disparagement

The Ambassador agrees not to knowingly make false or malicious statements intended to damage the reputation of Nepali Leaders Network, SKS Ventures LLC, Organization leadership, volunteers, Ambassadors, sponsors, partners, speakers, or community members. Nothing in this Agreement prohibits truthful statements required by law.

18Insurance

The Organization does not provide health, medical, automobile, liability, or other personal insurance coverage for Ambassadors. Each Ambassador is responsible for maintaining any insurance they consider appropriate.

19Termination

The Organization may immediately terminate this Agreement if the Ambassador fails to fulfill responsibilities, repeatedly fails to communicate, breaches confidentiality, violates Organization policies, damages the Organization’s reputation, misuses the Ambassador position, engages in misconduct, violates applicable law, or creates an unsafe environment.

The Ambassador may resign by providing reasonable notice. Upon termination, the Ambassador shall immediately cease representing NLN and discontinue use of the NLN Ambassador title, branding, logos, email signatures, and any representation that they continue to act on behalf of the Organization.

20Return of Organization Property

Upon completion or termination, the Ambassador shall immediately return or relinquish access to all Organization property, including accounts, passwords, files, documents, templates, equipment, contact lists, databases, marketing materials, event assets, and confidential information. Any Organization materials stored on personal devices shall be permanently deleted unless otherwise authorized.

21General Legal Terms

This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the Commonwealth of Massachusetts. Any modification to this Agreement must be in writing and signed by both parties. If any provision is determined to be unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect. Electronic signatures shall have the same legal effect as original signatures. Participation as an Ambassador does not guarantee future employment, contractor opportunities, partnerships, board positions, speaking engagements, references, or continued appointment. This Agreement, together with the applicable Role Addendum, constitutes the entire agreement between the parties regarding the Ambassador’s participation with NLN.

This Role Addendum (“Addendum”) forms part of the Master Volunteer Ambassador Agreement (“Agreement”) between Nepali Leaders Network (“NLN”), operated by SKS Ventures LLC (“Organization”), and the Ambassador (“Chapter Lead”). Unless otherwise stated, all terms of the Master Agreement remain in full force and effect.

The Chapter Lead serves as the primary leader and representative of NLN within their assigned city and is responsible for leading the local Ambassador team, driving chapter growth, building strategic relationships, and ensuring the successful execution of NLN’s signature networking events. The Chapter Lead reports directly to NLN Headquarters.

2Purpose of the Role

The purpose of the Chapter Lead role is to establish and grow a thriving NLN chapter that consistently delivers a premium member experience while strengthening NLN’s presence within the local Nepali professional community. The Chapter Lead is expected to lead with professionalism, ownership, initiative, and accountability.

3Core Responsibilities

A · Chapter Leadership

  • Leading the local Ambassador team and supporting the Operations Lead and Media Lead.
  • Delegating responsibilities appropriately and running weekly chapter meetings.
  • Monitoring progress toward chapter goals and ensuring deadlines and commitments are met.
  • Serving as the primary liaison between Headquarters and the local chapter.

B · Event Leadership

  • Leading overall event planning, timelines, and coordination meetings.
  • Assigning responsibilities, monitoring readiness, and overseeing event execution.
  • Ensuring a professional attendee experience and leading post-event debriefs.

The Chapter Lead is expected to successfully execute four (4) official NLN networking mixers during the appointment period, unless otherwise determined by Headquarters.

C · Partnerships & Speaker Development

  • Identifying potential speakers and conducting outreach to local professionals.
  • Building relationships with community leaders and coordinating speaker invitations.
  • Supporting sponsor and partnership conversations in coordination with Headquarters.

Final approval of speakers, sponsorships, partnerships, and major collaborations remains with Headquarters unless otherwise delegated.

D · Chapter Growth

  • Promoting NLN within the local community and recruiting attendees for events.
  • Identifying future volunteers and Ambassadors and strengthening member engagement.
  • Helping grow chapter visibility and attendance over time.

E · Communication with Headquarters

  • Attend required meetings and provide regular chapter updates.
  • Escalate issues requiring Headquarters’ support and communicate challenges proactively.
  • Implement Organization initiatives and feedback.

4Leadership Expectations

  • Lead by example and foster a positive, collaborative team culture.
  • Communicate respectfully and professionally.
  • Take initiative without waiting for direction on routine matters.
  • Make thoughtful decisions within the authority granted by the Organization.
  • Maintain a solutions-oriented mindset and uphold NLN’s mission, values, and reputation.

5Decision-Making Authority

The Chapter Lead may coordinate and manage routine chapter operations within Organization policies. However, the Chapter Lead may not, without prior written approval from Headquarters:

  • Enter into contracts or agreements, commit Organization funds, or approve expenditures.
  • Promise reimbursements or accept sponsorships or donations.
  • Launch new Organization initiatives or announce official partnerships.
  • Establish Organization policy or speak on behalf of NLN regarding legal, financial, or strategic matters.

6Performance Expectations

Performance may be evaluated based on factors including leadership and professionalism; reliability and responsiveness; communication with Headquarters; successful execution of chapter events; team leadership and collaboration; community engagement; attendance growth; speaker and partnership development; compliance with Organization policies; and overall contribution to the growth and success of the local chapter.

Performance evaluations are intended to support development and ensure consistency across all NLN chapters.

7Succession & Transition

If the Chapter Lead resigns, becomes inactive, or is removed from the role, the Chapter Lead agrees to fully cooperate in transferring chapter operations to Headquarters or the designated successor. This includes promptly transferring access to all Organization accounts, documents, contacts, event materials, and other Organization property associated with the chapter.

Unless otherwise stated, all terms of the Master Agreement remain in full force and effect.

1Appointment

The Operations Lead is responsible for planning, coordinating, and executing the operational aspects of the local NLN chapter, including venue coordination, event logistics, project management, and ensuring that every event is executed smoothly and professionally. The Operations Lead reports to the Chapter Lead and works closely with the Media Lead and NLN Headquarters.

2Purpose of the Role

The purpose of the Operations Lead role is to ensure that every NLN event is organized, efficient, and executed to a high standard. The Operations Lead serves as the operational backbone of the local chapter by coordinating logistics, tracking progress, and ensuring that plans are successfully executed.

3Core Responsibilities

A · Event Operations

  • Developing and maintaining event timelines and coordinating event logistics.
  • Managing attendee registration and check-in and preparing event materials and supplies.
  • Coordinating volunteers and event support and overseeing event setup and breakdown.
  • Monitoring the run-of-show, troubleshooting operational issues, and supporting a seamless attendee experience.

The Operations Lead is expected to support the successful execution of four (4) official NLN networking mixers during the appointment period, unless otherwise determined by Headquarters.

B · Venue Management

  • Identifying potential venues and conducting venue research and outreach.
  • Coordinating venue logistics and confirming setup requirements.
  • Ensuring venue policies are followed and coordinating with venue staff before, during, and after events.

The Operations Lead may assist with venue negotiations but shall not enter into venue agreements or make financial commitments on behalf of NLN without prior written approval from Headquarters.

C · Project Management

  • Tracking action items, monitoring deadlines, and following up on outstanding tasks.
  • Maintaining project management systems designated by NLN.
  • Keeping the chapter leadership team informed and escalating operational risks or delays when appropriate.

D · Chapter Support

  • Collaborating with the Chapter Lead and Media Lead.
  • Assisting with community-building initiatives and supporting attendee growth.
  • Helping improve chapter operations and contributing ideas that improve event quality and efficiency.

E · Communication with Headquarters

  • Attend required meetings and training sessions and communicate operational updates in a timely manner.
  • Notify Headquarters or the Chapter Lead of operational challenges that may affect event execution.
  • Implement operational systems and procedures established by Headquarters.

4Operational Expectations

  • Be highly organized and detail-oriented.
  • Take initiative to solve problems before they escalate.
  • Communicate proactively and professionally.
  • Maintain accurate records and timelines and remain calm under pressure during live events.
  • Execute responsibilities with consistency and reliability and uphold NLN’s mission, values, and standards.

5Decision-Making Authority

The Operations Lead may coordinate day-to-day operational activities within Organization policies. However, the Operations Lead may not, without prior written approval from Headquarters:

  • Enter into contracts or agreements, commit Organization funds, or approve expenditures.
  • Promise reimbursements, accept sponsorships or donations, or approve venue agreements.
  • Launch Organization initiatives or establish Organization policy.
  • Make legal or financial commitments on behalf of NLN.

6Performance Expectations

Performance may be evaluated based on factors including organization and attention to detail; reliability and responsiveness; communication with the chapter leadership team and Headquarters; successful execution of event logistics; timely completion of operational responsibilities; ability to troubleshoot and solve problems; compliance with Organization policies; and contribution to the growth and success of the local chapter.

Performance evaluations are intended to support development and maintain operational consistency across all NLN chapters.

7Succession & Transition

If the Operations Lead resigns, becomes inactive, or is removed from the role, the Operations Lead agrees to fully cooperate in transferring operational responsibilities to Headquarters or the designated successor, including promptly transferring all Organization documents, operational plans, venue information, project management records, event materials, and other Organization property associated with the chapter.

The Media Lead is responsible for leading the local marketing, communications, and content efforts for the assigned NLN chapter. The Media Lead works closely with the Chapter Lead, Operations Lead, and NLN Headquarters to promote chapter events, increase community engagement, and capture high-quality media that strengthens the NLN brand. The Media Lead reports to the Chapter Lead and collaborates closely with NLN Headquarters on marketing initiatives.

2Purpose of the Role

The purpose of the Media Lead role is to increase awareness of NLN within the local community by executing high-quality event marketing, creating engaging promotional content, and documenting each chapter event through professional photo and video coverage. The Media Lead plays a critical role in growing event attendance, strengthening NLN’s brand presence, and showcasing the impact of the local chapter.

3Core Responsibilities

A · Event Marketing

  • Creating and executing local event promotion plans.
  • Developing promotional graphics and marketing assets, where applicable.
  • Publishing event announcements in accordance with NLN’s marketing guidelines.
  • Promoting speaker announcements and event updates and supporting attendee recruitment.
  • Encouraging community engagement before each event.

The Media Lead is expected to support the promotion of four (4) official NLN networking mixers during the appointment period, unless otherwise determined by Headquarters.

B · Content Creation & Live Event Coverage

  • Coordinating photo and video coverage and capturing professional event photography.
  • Recording high-quality video footage of attendee interactions, networking moments, speakers, and event highlights.
  • Ensuring all key moments identified by Headquarters are documented.
  • Organizing and securely storing raw media files until delivery.

The Media Lead may personally capture content or coordinate qualified volunteers or photographers, subject to Organization approval.

C · Content Delivery to Headquarters

  • Uploading all raw photo and video files and organizing them according to NLN’s file structure.
  • Delivering media by the deadlines established by Headquarters.
  • Communicating any missing or incomplete content promptly.

NLN Headquarters will be responsible for post-event editing, recap videos, highlight reels, and final content publishing unless otherwise directed.

D · Chapter Growth

  • Promoting NLN within the local community and increasing awareness of upcoming events.
  • Supporting event attendance growth and encouraging community engagement across approved channels.
  • Contributing creative ideas that strengthen NLN’s local presence and brand visibility.

E · Communication with Headquarters

  • Attend required meetings and training sessions and coordinate with Headquarters on marketing campaigns.
  • Follow NLN brand guidelines and content standards.
  • Seek approval when required and communicate progress, challenges, and content needs in a timely manner.

4Brand & Content Expectations

  • Represent the NLN brand professionally and consistently.
  • Follow NLN’s brand guidelines, messaging, and visual identity.
  • Maintain high standards for photography, videography, and promotional materials.
  • Ensure content reflects the professionalism and quality of NLN events.
  • Protect confidential information and respect the privacy of attendees in compliance with Organization policies.

5Decision-Making Authority

The Media Lead may coordinate local marketing and content initiatives within Organization policies. However, the Media Lead may not, without prior written approval from Headquarters:

  • Create or modify official brand guidelines or launch independent marketing campaigns outside Organization strategy.
  • Issue official public statements on behalf of NLN or announce partnerships or sponsorships.
  • Commit Organization funds, approve expenditures, or enter into contracts with photographers, videographers, vendors, or agencies.
  • Make legal or financial commitments on behalf of NLN.

6Performance Expectations

Performance may be evaluated based on factors including quality and professionalism of promotional content; consistency in executing local marketing efforts; timely delivery of promotional assets and event media; compliance with NLN branding and communication standards; creativity and initiative; reliability and responsiveness; collaboration with the chapter leadership team and Headquarters; and contribution to event attendance, chapter visibility, and community engagement.

Performance evaluations are intended to support development and maintain consistent branding across all NLN chapters.

7Succession & Transition

If the Media Lead resigns, becomes inactive, or is removed from the role, the Media Lead agrees to fully cooperate in transferring all Organization-owned marketing assets, media files, design files, project files, social media access, documents, and other Organization property to Headquarters or the designated successor.
